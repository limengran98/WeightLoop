# Third-party notices

## Figure typefaces

The paper figures contain embedded subsets of Comic Neue, STIX General and
Linux Libertine typefaces. Their licenses permit embedding. The Comic Neue
OFL notice is retained in `licenses/ComicNeue-OFL.txt`; the STIX and Libertine
notices are retained alongside it. No font needs to be downloaded to view the
project page, which uses system fonts.

## Scientific models and source data

The paper uses publicly available scientific datasets and model interfaces,
including GEARS, PhaseNet, ClimaX and FNO2d, alongside retained scGPT and scFoundation calibration records. Their papers and source terms are
referenced in the manuscript and reproduction guide. This repository does not
vendor their pretrained weights or declare them MIT-licensed. scFoundation
code and pretrained-weight terms are separate.

## Related work

Weight-space programming and WeightCode are cited in the manuscript. The
repository and website presentation reference their research-project format;
WeightCode software, text and artwork have not been copied into this release.
